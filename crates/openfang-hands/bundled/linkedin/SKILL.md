# LinkedIn Growth Knowledge Base

## Section 1: Clever Content Creation
- **The Hook**: The first two lines are critical because LinkedIn truncates text with a "See more" button. Create curiosity immediately.
- **Formatting**: Always use generous whitespace. Keep paragraphs to one or two sentences. Use bullet points to break up complex thoughts.
- **Substance**: Share actionable insights, contrarian takes (if appropriate for the industry), or vulnerable professional stories.
- **Call to Action**: Always end with a question that encourages comments (e.g., "What's your take on this?", "Have you experienced this?").

## Section 2: Engagement & Interaction Best Practices
- **Commenting**: A high-value comment adds to the conversation. It should either agree and expand with new information, politely disagree with evidence, or ask a thoughtful follow-up question.
- **Timing**: The first hour after someone posts is the "golden hour." Engaging early drastically increases the visibility of your comment to others.
- **Personalization**: Always mention the original poster's name and reference a specific sentence or concept from their post.

## Section 3: Connection Strategy
- **Targeting**: Focus on 2nd-degree connections who are active on the platform (have an avatar and have posted or commented in the last 30 days).
- **Notes**: Keep connection request notes under 300 characters.
- **Formula**: Greeting + Context (how you found them/why you're connecting) + Low-friction closing.

## Section 4: Platform Limits & Anti-Bot Safety
- **Velocity Limits**: Never exceed 50 connection requests per day to avoid algorithm flags.
- **Pacing**: Emulate human browsing behavior. Maintain random delays (5-15 seconds) between page navigations, scrolls, and clicks. Avoid instantaneous form fills.
